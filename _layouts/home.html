I develop alchemical free energy methods for the covalent chemistry of proteins (protonation, disulfide bonds and post-translational modifications) and apply them to aquaporins, disordered proteins, and amyloids.

Postdoctoral researcher in Bert de Groot's group at the Max Planck Institute for Multidisciplinary Sciences. Next: combining computational PTM thermodynamics with cryo-EM of amyloid fibrils.
